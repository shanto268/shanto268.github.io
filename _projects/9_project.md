## HiperWalk

[Hiperwalk](https://hiperwalk.org/docs/stable/index.html), an acronym for High-Performance Quantum Walk Simulator, is a powerful, open-source software designed to simulate quantum walks on various graph structures. Leveraging heterogeneous High-Performance Computing (HPC),

**GitHub Repository**: [HiperWalk](https://github.com/hiperwalk/hiperwalk/)

**Documentation**: [HiperWalk Documentation](https://hiperwalk.org/docs/stable/index.html)

As a part of my capstone project, I have worked on this codebase. I have contributed to the development of the codebase, including migrating the codebase from C/C++ to python, refactoring it to be HPC native, added to the documentation, and testing of the codebase. I also authored the discrete-time `QuantumWalk` class for the very first release.
