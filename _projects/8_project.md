This was one of my favorite classes at TTU—a chance to relive the greatest hits of physics history, but with our own hands. We got to re-run classic experiments, test fundamental principles, and then go off-script with a few fun projects of our own.

It wasn’t just about the physics (though that part was great)—it was also about late nights in the lab, debugging circuits with friends, and the thrill of getting real data from something we built ourselves.

Here are a few repositories from that time that bring back good memories:

- [Solar Panel Project](https://github.com/shanto268/solar_panel_project)
- [Mobile Sensor Project](https://github.com/shanto268/mobile_sensor_project)
- [Rotational EMF Lab](https://github.com/shanto268/Rotational-EMF-Lab)
- [Muon Lifetime Experiment](https://github.com/shanto268/MuonLifetime)
- [Torsional Oscillator](https://github.com/shanto268/TorsoinalOscillator)
- [Fourier Methods](https://github.com/shanto268/FourierMethods)

Physics is always better with good company.
