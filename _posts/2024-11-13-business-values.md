There is no doubt in my mind that one day I will be running my own business. I really don't know how to rationalize why I would want to do so but it really does feel like a calling that I need to address. Here is a list of values (hopefully growing one) that I have pondered over the years that I would like to implement in my business one day from day one:

- Never punish initiatives - reward them!
- Liberate workers with ownership of work
- We take pride in what we build
- We do it because no one else can do it and it needs to be done
- We will always encourage you to follow your dreams and passions - even if it hurts us
- Optimistic in everything
- Be kind to each other 
- We believe in the greater good
- Never stop innovation, never stop iterations
- Authority can and should be questioned
- Automation and delegation of tasks are rewarded as long as they get done
- Always use the latest tools. Integrate only stable and secure parts
- Recruitment and culture is what defines an institution 
- Promotions/power should never be given to someone solely on the basis that they possess information they are not willing to share
- deadlines = deadlines $$/ \pi$$ for MVPs. The rest of the time -> we have fun with the product
- One shall receive $$1/\pi$$ times any asymmetry revenue flow one creates
- Chain of commands are important - only mess with it if you have a better sequence of succession
- Institutions before individuals
- Make products/services that bring people joy
- Create products that have cultural significance
- Create systems that motivate fully transparent communication between employees and the executives
- Always give the customers more than what they pay for
- If we work on services/products that democratizes privilege we will always have a market 
- We recognize great value is created on top of networks
- We would much rather starve than serve the enemies of America
- We want "busy bees" - folks whose task is to explore random opportunities for no short term gain but simply to get exposure to positive luck
- If we are providing a service where women and children don't feel safe - WE ARE DOING THINGS WRONG!
- My company and its constituents will come first over me. ALWAYS.

Please remember, these are just my personal thoughts at time $$t$$, where $$t$$ denotes the current point in my mental timeline, subject to stochastic jumps, non-Markovian memory effects, and frequent Bayesian updates. All opinions are therefore functions of $$t$$ and may be discontinuous and non-differentiable. They don’t represent the views of any organization, past, present, or future, and shouldn’t be taken as advice or policy. I’ll do my best to keep this list updated as I grow and change myself :)
